THE PERSON BEHIND SABI

A different mind.
A place to be yourself.

I know what it’s like to have ideas, ambition and ability, alongside limited energy and a brain that doesn’t always follow the timetable everyone else seems to have been issued.

SABI grew from noticing how often people are made to feel they’re failing when the thing around them simply doesn’t fit. That understanding runs through my work. Studio is where it takes a more creative shape.

Here, it might become a ghost wearing headphones, a row of masks, or a winding route that takes in every interesting distraction along the way. There’s humour in those things, but there’s recognition too.

I want someone to find a design and feel understood. Neurodivergent people are part of this story because I am one. So are people who have always felt a bit different, and people who just enjoy something whimsical and a little weird.

There’s room for all of that here.Sam x

THE DETAILS MATTER TOO

Comfort is personal.

A lovely design is only part of it.
You need to know how it will feel on you.

01

How it feels

Fabric, texture, stretch and where the print sits. “Soft” means different things to different people, so we want to give you details you can actually use.

02

How it fits

Measurements to compare with something you already like wearing. A size label on its own can only tell you so much.

03

How warm it is

The weight of the fabric and how it layers. A heavyweight hoodie and a cropped tee do very different jobs.

We’re checking these details for each launch garment. They’ll be on the product pages before you can order. If there’s something particular you need to know, ask Sam.

Say hello to Sam ↗
Do I have to be neurodivergent to wear SABI?

No. The designs grow out of neurodivergent experience, but you don’t need a diagnosis or an explanation to enjoy them. If something feels like you, or you simply like it, you’re welcome.

Can I order yet?

Not quite. This is a preview while the first collection is being prepared. Prices, final garment details and ordering will be added when everything is ready.

How will my order be made?

The plan is to make each piece to order with our print partners. The mask clothing and tote are being prepared with Inkthreadable, the mini print with Prodigi, and the A4 print with dStudio. The Ghost Mode range is planned through Printful. We’ll explain production and delivery times before you order.

What about delivery and returns?

We’re finalising those details. Delivery costs, time estimates and the returns policy will be available before the shop opens, so you can read them before deciding.

I have a question about fit or feel.

Email info@sabigroup.co.uk with the piece you’re interested in and what you’d like to know. We’ll tell you what we know and what still needs checking.
