1. Information SABI may collect

SABI may collect:

  • name, contact details and communication preferences;
  • payment and contract records, including the service purchased, payment status, cancellation choice and transaction reference;
  • work history, education, qualifications, training, volunteering, caring, projects and other relevant experience;
  • skills, achievements, interests, values, working preferences and career goals;
  • target roles, vacancies, practical requirements and deadlines;
  • CVs, applications, cover letters, job adverts and other documents supplied for the service;
  • follow-up answers, consultation notes, feedback, revisions and final approvals; and
  • optional information about accessibility, communication, disability, health or neurodivergence where the client chooses to provide it.

SABI does not ask clients to send passwords, verification codes or full payment-card details. Detailed health or diagnosis information is optional, and practical needs can be described without naming a condition.

2. Why the information is used

SABI uses information to:

  • set up and administer the contract;
  • process payment, refunds and accounting records;
  • understand the client’s experience and career goals;
  • review documents, ask follow-up questions and deliver the agreed Career Partner work;
  • adapt communication or delivery where reasonably needed;
  • provide drafts, revisions, follow-up support and replacement copies;
  • protect accounts, files and systems; and
  • respond to complaints, legal obligations and data-protection requests.

The main legal bases are contract, steps requested before entering a contract, legal obligation and legitimate interests. Where the client voluntarily provides health, disability or neurodivergence information, SABI also asks for separate explicit consent before using that special-category information.

3. Optional sensitive information

Questions about health, disability, accessibility or neurodivergence are optional. Leaving them blank does not prevent access to the ordinary service.

SABI will not place this information in a CV, application, cover letter or other career document without separate agreement. Consent may be withdrawn for future optional use by contacting the privacy email. Limited information may still need to be retained where it remains necessary for an active service, complaint, legal claim or legal obligation.

4. Systems and service providers

SABI currently intends to use:

  • Stripe for payment processing. Stripe receives card and transaction information and may also use information for fraud prevention, regulatory and security purposes;
  • Google Workspace for business email, restricted form processing, private document storage, internal records and delivery;
  • Netlify to host the client-facing website pages; and
  • GitHub for website source code only. Client submissions, uploaded documents, credentials and private service records must not be stored in the repository.

Calls and meetings are not automatically recorded. No recording or transcription provider is approved for this service unless a specific recording is proposed, assessed and separately agreed.

No AI provider is approved for identifiable client information merely because AI-assisted working may be useful. Any identifiable use requires a provider, account, settings and permitted data flow to be assessed and approved first. SABI remains responsible for every client-facing output and does not make solely automated decisions about career direction or final documents.

5. Sharing and international processing

SABI does not sell personal information. Information is shared only where reasonably necessary with approved service providers, professional advisers, insurers, banks, regulators, courts, law-enforcement bodies, emergency services or safeguarding organisations where lawful.

Some providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. SABI will rely on the relevant provider terms and recognised safeguards and must complete the final account-level and transfer checks before payment opens.

6. How long information is kept

Ordinary Career Partner working files are normally kept for 12 months after the service is formally completed or ended. A smaller contract, payment, complaint and financial record may be kept for up to six years where needed for tax, accounting, legal claims or evidence of the agreement.

Raw call or meeting recordings, where separately agreed, will be deleted once useful information has been transferred where practical and no later than three months after the contract ends unless an active complaint, dispute, legal claim or legal duty requires longer retention.

7. The client’s rights

Depending on the circumstances, the client may ask SABI to:

  • provide access to personal information;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • delete information;
  • restrict or object to certain uses;
  • provide information in a portable format; or
  • record withdrawal of consent for future optional processing.

Some rights depend on the legal basis and may be limited where information must be kept for an active contract, legal claim, tax record or other legal obligation.

Privacy requests can be sent to privacy@sabigroup.co.uk. The client may also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

8. Security and changes

SABI uses restricted business accounts, access controls, private storage and proportionate security measures. Clients should not send passwords, security answers, verification codes or full card details.

This notice may be updated where systems, providers or legal requirements change. Material changes affecting an active service will be explained rather than silently applied.
