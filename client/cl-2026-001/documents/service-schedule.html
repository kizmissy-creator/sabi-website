1. Basis of this schedule

This schedule records the personalised Career Partner package offered to Bronagh in her Career Support Proposal. It is specific to Bronagh and is not the narrower Career Partner package later developed for SABI’s public launch.

The Service Schedule, booking confirmation, applicable Career Support Terms and Privacy Notice together record the agreed service.

2. Included service

Career consultation and information gathering

The service will use a written-first consultation process. SABI will review Bronagh’s onboarding form and supporting documents, then send focused written follow-up questions where more detail, evidence or clarification is needed.

A telephone or video call of up to 30 minutes may be arranged where useful to discuss recommendations, compare career options or confirm decisions. Bronagh may continue entirely through the written route where preferred.

Career Direction and Job Search Plan

Bronagh will receive one coordinated document containing:

  • a Career Direction Summary
  • a Transferable Skills Analysis
  • suitable role and industry recommendations
  • comparison of realistic options
  • a recommended primary direction
  • a personalised job-search strategy
  • useful job titles and search terms
  • advice on where and how to search
  • relevant barriers, development needs and practical next steps

Core CV

Bronagh will receive one professionally written core CV positioned towards her agreed general career direction or industry.

Additional targeted CV and cover letter

Bronagh will receive one additional CV tailored to one agreed vacancy, role type or career path, together with one matching tailored cover letter. Where no live vacancy is available, the documents may be positioned towards one clearly defined target agreed with Bronagh.

A substantially unrelated second career direction is not included unless separately agreed.

Application review

The package includes one review of a substantially completed application, supporting statement or set of application answers for one agreed vacancy, normally up to approximately 1,000 words.

The review includes feedback and reasonable edits to improve relevance, evidence, structure and clarity, followed by one minor pre-submission check. Substantial drafting from the beginning or unusually complex applications may require separate agreement.

Written-first interview preparation

The package includes personalised written interview preparation for one agreed vacancy, role type or closely related career direction.

This may include:

  • likely question themes
  • suitable evidence from Bronagh’s experience
  • guidance on answer structure
  • points to communicate clearly
  • practical preparation
  • questions to ask the employer
  • reasonable-adjustment considerations where relevant

A telephone or video discussion of up to 30 minutes may be arranged where useful to talk through the written guidance or explore particular answers.

A formal mock interview and repeated coaching sessions are not included unless separately agreed.

Follow-up support

The package includes two weeks of reasonable follow-up support by email or WhatsApp.

Bronagh may choose for the support period to begin on any date within 30 calendar days after delivery of the final main documents. If she uses the support before choosing a date, the support period begins on that date.

The support covers clarification and brief guidance connected to the completed work. It does not include substantial new documents, further full application reviews, extensive new research or support for a substantially different career direction.

3. File formats

The core CV, additional targeted CV and tailored cover letter will be supplied in editable Word and ready-to-use PDF formats.

The Career Direction and Job Search Plan and written interview preparation will be supplied in an appropriate accessible written format. Alternative formats may be agreed where reasonably required.

4. Revision allowance

Each main written deliverable includes one consolidated revision round and a final minor-corrections check.

This applies to:

  • the core CV
  • the additional targeted CV
  • the Career Direction and Job Search Plan
  • the tailored cover letter
  • the written interview preparation

Feedback should normally be provided together for each document. New information, a substantially different career direction or extensive rewriting requested after approval may require separate agreement.

The application review has its own agreed process: one full review followed by one minor pre-submission check.

5. Delivery process and normal timescales

The service will normally be delivered in stages:

  1. Onboarding form and supporting documents received.
  2. SABI reviews the information and sends focused written follow-up questions.
  3. Career options, transferable skills and the primary direction are analysed and agreed.
  4. The Career Direction and Job Search Plan and core CV are drafted.
  5. Bronagh reviews the drafts and provides consolidated feedback.
  6. Revisions are completed.
  7. The target for the additional CV and cover letter is confirmed.
  8. The targeted CV and matching cover letter are prepared and delivered.
  9. Follow-up support is activated when Bronagh chooses.
  10. The application review and interview preparation are used when required within the agreed availability period.

SABI will normally review the completed onboarding form and supporting documents and send any focused follow-up questions within five working days of the service starting.

First main drafts will normally be provided within ten working days after SABI has received the information and decisions needed to begin drafting.

Where information, feedback or a suitable target vacancy is still needed, the relevant timescale will pause until it is supplied. Revised timescales will be explained where necessary.

No urgent external deadline is accepted unless SABI confirms it in writing.

6. Application and interview support availability

The included application review and written interview preparation may each be used once for a suitable vacancy within three calendar months after the final main documents are delivered.

Bronagh must provide the vacancy information and relevant materials with enough time for SABI to complete the work before the employer’s deadline.

An extension may be agreed where illness, accessibility needs or other reasonable circumstances prevented use within that period.

7. Information Bronagh needs to provide

Bronagh should provide any existing CVs and the information reasonably needed to complete the service. This may include:

  • employment dates and role details
  • qualifications and training
  • achievements and useful examples
  • previous applications or cover letters
  • relevant job descriptions
  • vacancies or career ideas she is considering
  • practical, communication or accessibility requirements she would like SABI to consider

Bronagh is not expected to have every document or know all the answers before beginning. SABI may use focused written questions to fill gaps and clarify information.

For vacancy-specific work, Bronagh should also provide the vacancy advert, person specification and employer instructions where available.

Bronagh remains responsible for checking the factual accuracy of final documents and confirming that she is comfortable with the wording before using them.

8. Service start and cancellation period

The service begins once:

  • the onboarding form and payment have been received
  • the required service documents have been accepted
  • SABI is permitted to begin under the recorded cancellation and early-start arrangements

The initial review timescale begins from the service-start date. Drafting timescales begin once SABI has the essential information and decisions needed to produce the relevant documents.

Bronagh may separately ask SABI to begin work during the 14-day cancellation period. If she cancels after work has begun, SABI may deduct a fair and proportionate amount reflecting work already completed.

If Bronagh does not request an early start, substantive work will begin after the cancellation period ends.

9. Communication and call notes

Routine communication will normally take place by email. WhatsApp will be available during the included follow-up period and for brief practical messages.

Detailed information, document feedback and important decisions should normally be sent by email or through the agreed written system so that both parties have a reliable record.

Calls will not normally be recorded. Any recording requires advance notice and Bronagh’s separate agreement. Declining a recording will not affect the service.

SABI may make proportionate written notes of relevant information and agreed decisions.

10. Exclusions and boundaries

The £135 package does not include:

  • weekly vacancy suggestions, unless separately purchased or agreed
  • unlimited messaging or continuous availability
  • repeated full application reviews
  • repeated interview-coaching sessions
  • a formal mock interview
  • new CVs or cover letters for further unrelated directions
  • ongoing vacancy searching
  • substantial new documents outside the agreed package
  • guarantees of interviews, job offers, salary or employment outcomes

Weekly Job Suggestions were presented as an optional additional service and are not included in the £135 package.

11. Completion

The main-document stage is completed when Bronagh’s final Career Direction and Job Search Plan, core CV, targeted CV and tailored cover letter have been delivered.

The included application review and written interview preparation remain available for three calendar months from that date.

The full service ends when all included elements have been completed or when the three-month availability period expires, whichever occurs first.

The two-week follow-up period may run during this time according to its agreed activation arrangements.
