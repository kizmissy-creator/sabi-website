1. What you are buying

This agreement replaces the separate proposal and Service Schedule for the purpose of this purchase. It records the bespoke £135 package selected after the Career Support Proposal dated 15 July 2026. If the earlier proposal and this agreement differ, this agreement records the final scope.

The package includes:

  • a written-first review of the onboarding form and supporting documents;
  • focused written follow-up questions where more detail, evidence or clarification is needed;
  • one Career Direction and Job Search Plan, including transferable-skills analysis, realistic role or industry options, a recommended direction, search terms and practical next steps;
  • one professionally written core CV;
  • one additional CV tailored to one agreed vacancy, role type or clearly defined career direction;
  • one matching tailored cover letter;
  • one review of a substantially completed application, supporting statement or set of answers for one agreed vacancy, normally up to approximately 1,000 words, followed by one minor pre-submission check;
  • personalised written interview preparation for one agreed vacancy, role type or closely related direction, with an optional telephone or video discussion of up to 30 minutes where useful; and
  • two weeks of reasonable follow-up support by email or WhatsApp, beginning on a date chosen within 30 days after the final main documents are delivered. A qualifying support request made before a date is chosen starts the two-week period.

2. How the service will work

The service is written-first. A call is optional rather than compulsory. SABI may suggest a telephone or video discussion of up to 30 minutes where it would help compare options, confirm decisions or talk through interview preparation. The client may continue entirely in writing where preferred.

SABI will normally review a completed onboarding form and supporting documents and send any first focused follow-up questions within five working days after substantive work is permitted to begin.

First main drafts will normally be provided within ten working days after SABI has the information and decisions reasonably needed to begin drafting. A multi-document package may be delivered in stages. Timescales pause while SABI is waiting for information, documents, decisions, feedback or a suitable target vacancy.

No urgent external deadline is accepted unless SABI confirms it in writing.

3. Revisions and final checks

Each main written deliverable includes one consolidated revision round and one final minor-corrections check. This applies to the Career Direction and Job Search Plan, core CV, targeted CV, cover letter and written interview preparation.

The application review has its own process: one full review followed by one minor pre-submission check.

Errors introduced by SABI do not use the revision allowance. A substantially different career direction, a new vacancy document, materially new information supplied after drafting or another new document may require separate agreement and an additional fee. SABI will explain any proposed charge before carrying out extra work.

4. What the client needs to do

The client should provide information and documents that are accurate and reasonably complete to the best of their knowledge. This may include employment dates, role details, qualifications, achievements, previous applications, relevant job descriptions and any vacancy or career ideas being considered.

The client is not expected to remember everything immediately. Important corrections or new information should be supplied as soon as reasonably possible.

The client must check final documents before using them, particularly names, contact details, employers, dates, qualifications, licences and factual claims. SABI will not knowingly invent, fabricate or materially misrepresent experience or evidence.

5. What is not included

The package does not include weekly vacancy suggestions, ongoing vacancy searching, unlimited messaging, continuous availability, repeated full application reviews, repeated interview-coaching sessions, a formal mock interview, or further CVs and cover letters for unrelated directions unless separately agreed.

SABI is not an employer or recruitment agency and cannot guarantee an interview, job offer, salary or other employment outcome. SABI will provide the agreed service with reasonable care and skill.

6. Payment and formation of the contract

The total price is £135. It is a one-time payment and does not renew automatically. Stripe handles card processing. SABI does not receive or store full payment-card details.

The contract is formed when payment succeeds and SABI issues a booking or contract confirmation. That confirmation will provide a copy of the agreed information in a form the client can save.

An automated payment receipt does not mean substantive personalised work has already begun.

7. Cancellation and early start

A consumer purchasing this service online normally has 14 days from the day after the contract is formed to cancel without giving a reason. Cancellation can be made by sending SABI a clear statement. The cancellation form may be used but is not compulsory.

SABI will not begin substantive personalised work during the cancellation period unless the client separately asks for an early start.

If the client requests an early start and later cancels before the service is complete, SABI may deduct only a fair and proportionate amount for work actually supplied before cancellation. If the required request and acknowledgement have been given and the service is fully performed during the cancellation period, the statutory cancellation right may end once full performance is complete.

If the client does not request an early start, substantive work will begin after the cancellation period ends. If cancellation takes place before substantive work begins, the payment will be refunded in full. A statutory cancellation refund will normally be made within 14 days after SABI is informed, using the original payment method unless another lawful method is agreed.

8. Communication, calls and recordings

Routine communication will normally take place by email. WhatsApp may be used during the included follow-up period and for brief practical messages. Detailed information, document feedback and important decisions should normally be sent by email or through the agreed written system so both parties have a reliable record.

Calls and video meetings are not automatically recorded. Any recording requires advance notice and the client’s separate agreement. Declining a recording does not reduce the service.

9. Ownership and use of final documents

The client keeps their rights in original information and material they provide. SABI may use that material only as reasonably needed to deliver and administer the service and meet legal or record-keeping duties.

SABI retains its rights in original templates, layouts, questionnaires, checklists, general guidance, branding and service methods. Once the agreed price has been paid, the client may permanently use, copy, store, print, submit, edit and adapt the final personalised career documents for their own employment, education, volunteering and career-development purposes.

10. Pauses, ending the service and concerns

Work may be paused where necessary because essential information is missing, the client has not responded, illness or emergency affects delivery, or a significant security or operational issue arises. SABI will explain the reason and what is needed to continue where reasonably possible.

After 30 calendar days without a necessary response, despite reasonable attempts to make contact, SABI may mark the matter as paused. Pausing or ending a matter does not automatically make the full payment non-refundable. Any refund or retained amount will be assessed fairly according to the work supplied and the client’s legal rights.

Questions or complaints should be sent to hello@sabigroup.co.uk. SABI will acknowledge the concern, review the relevant records and try to provide a clear response and fair remedy where something has gone wrong.

11. Privacy and final provisions

The separate Career Partner Privacy Notice explains what personal information SABI collects, why it is used, who it may be shared with, how long it is kept and the client’s rights. The Privacy Notice is information, not a contract or consent form.

Nothing in this agreement removes rights that cannot lawfully be excluded. This agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales, while any mandatory consumer right to bring proceedings elsewhere remains unaffected.
