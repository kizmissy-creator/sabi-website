Privacy Policy

This policy explains how personal information is used for the Career Partner service.

1. Information collected

SABI may collect contact details, payment and contract records, work and education history, qualifications, skills, achievements, career goals, vacancies, documents supplied for the service, feedback, revisions and optional accessibility or health information.

Detailed health, disability or neurodivergence information is optional. Practical needs may be described without naming a diagnosis. SABI does not ask clients to send passwords, verification codes or full card details.

2. How information is used

Information is used to administer the purchase, take payment, understand the client’s experience and goals, deliver the agreed service, adapt communication where needed, provide drafts and revisions, protect systems, keep necessary records and respond to complaints or legal obligations.

The main legal bases are contract, steps requested before entering a contract, legal obligation and legitimate interests. Where optional health or disability information is provided, SABI asks for separate explicit consent before using it.

3. Service providers

SABI intends to use Stripe for payment, Google Workspace for business email, restricted form processing and private document storage, Netlify for client-facing website pages, and GitHub for website source code only.

Client submissions, uploaded documents, credentials and private service records must not be stored in GitHub. Calls are not automatically recorded. No recording, transcription or AI provider may receive identifiable client information unless the provider, account, settings and data flow have first been assessed and approved.

4. Sharing and transfers

SABI does not sell personal information. Information is shared only where reasonably necessary with approved service providers, professional advisers, banks, insurers, regulators, courts, law-enforcement bodies, emergency services or safeguarding organisations where lawful.

Some providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. SABI will use the relevant provider terms and recognised safeguards and must complete final account-level checks before payment opens.

5. Retention

Ordinary Career Partner working files are normally kept for 12 months after the service is completed or ended. A smaller contract, payment, complaint and financial record may be kept for up to six years where needed for tax, accounting, legal claims or evidence of the agreement.

Any raw recording separately agreed for a call or meeting will normally be deleted once useful information has been transferred and no later than three months after the contract ends, unless an active dispute or legal duty requires longer retention.

6. Client rights

Depending on the circumstances, the client may ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the use of personal information, request portability or withdraw consent for future optional processing.

Requests can be sent to privacy@sabigroup.co.uk. The client may also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.
