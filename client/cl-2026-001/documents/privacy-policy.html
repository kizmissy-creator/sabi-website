SABI CAREER SUPPORT

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how personal information is used for the Career Partner service.

Controller: Samantha Ingram trading as SABI
Privacy email: privacy@sabigroup.co.uk
Business address: 136 Hareydene, Newcastle upon Tyne, NE5 4QP

1. Information collected

SABI may collect contact details, payment and contract records, work and education history, qualifications, skills, achievements, career goals, vacancies, documents supplied for the service, feedback and revisions.

Name, email address, payment or contract information, and enough career information to provide the agreed service are required. Without them, SABI may not be able to provide the service. Telephone number, pronouns, accessibility information and detailed health, disability or neurodivergence information are optional. Practical support needs can be described without naming a diagnosis.

SABI does not ask clients to send passwords, verification codes or full payment-card details.

2. How information is used and the lawful bases

Information needed to arrange, administer and deliver the Career Partner service is normally used because it is necessary to take steps at the client’s request before entering a contract or to perform the contract.

SABI also uses information where necessary to meet legal obligations, including relevant tax, accounting and regulatory requirements. Legitimate interests may be used for proportionate business administration, service security, fraud or misuse prevention, handling complaints and establishing or defending legal claims. These interests are balanced against the client’s rights and reasonable expectations.

Where a client chooses to provide health, disability or neurodivergence information, SABI asks for separate explicit consent before using that special-category information for accessibility or service adaptation. Consent can be withdrawn for future optional use at any time by contacting the privacy email.

3. Service providers and security

SABI uses Stripe for payment, Google Workspace for business email, restricted form processing and private document storage, and Netlify for client-facing website pages. GitHub is used for website source code only; client submissions, uploaded documents, credentials and private service records are not intended to be stored there.

Access to client information is restricted to what is reasonably needed to provide and administer the service. Calls and meetings are not automatically recorded. No recording, transcription or AI provider may receive identifiable client information unless the provider, account, settings and proposed data use have first been assessed and approved.

4. Sharing and international transfers

SABI does not sell personal information. Information is shared only where reasonably necessary with service providers or, where lawful and necessary, professional advisers, banks, insurers, regulators, courts, law-enforcement bodies, emergency services or safeguarding organisations.

Some service providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where a restricted international transfer occurs, SABI relies on an applicable UK adequacy regulation or appropriate contractual safeguards contained in the provider’s data-processing terms, such as recognised data-transfer addenda or standard contractual protections. Information about the safeguards used for a particular provider can be requested from privacy@sabigroup.co.uk.

5. Retention

Ordinary Career Partner working files are normally kept for 12 months after the service is completed or ended. A smaller contract, payment, complaint and financial record may be kept for up to six years where needed for tax, accounting, legal claims or evidence of the agreement.

Any raw recording separately agreed for a call or meeting will normally be deleted once useful information has been transferred and no later than three months after the contract ends, unless an active dispute or legal duty requires longer retention.

6. Automated decision-making

SABI does not make decisions about the client that have legal or similarly significant effects solely by automated means. Automated systems may support routine functions such as security, form handling or administrative processing, but career recommendations and client-facing work remain subject to human review.

7. Client rights

Depending on the circumstances and the lawful basis being used, the client may ask to access, correct, delete or restrict personal information, object to certain uses, request portability, or withdraw consent for future optional processing.

Requests can be sent to privacy@sabigroup.co.uk. The client may also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.
