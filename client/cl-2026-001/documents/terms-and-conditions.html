1. The service

The £135 Career Partner package includes:

  • a written-first review of the onboarding form and supporting documents;
  • focused follow-up questions where more detail is needed;
  • one Career Direction and Job Search Plan;
  • one core CV;
  • one additional CV tailored to one agreed vacancy, role type or clearly defined direction;
  • one matching tailored cover letter;
  • one review of a substantially completed application or supporting statement, normally up to approximately 1,000 words, followed by one minor pre-submission check;
  • personalised written interview preparation, with an optional telephone or video discussion of up to 30 minutes where useful; and
  • two weeks of reasonable follow-up support by email or WhatsApp.

The included application review and interview preparation may each be used once within three calendar months after the final main documents are delivered. The two-week follow-up period may begin on a date chosen within 30 days after those documents are delivered. Using the support before choosing a date starts the period on that day.

2. Delivery and revisions

The service is written-first. A call is optional. SABI will normally review the completed onboarding information and send first follow-up questions within five working days after work is permitted to begin.

First main drafts will normally be provided within ten working days after SABI has the information and decisions needed to begin drafting. The package may be delivered in stages. Timescales pause while SABI is waiting for information, documents, decisions, feedback or a suitable target vacancy. Urgent deadlines apply only where SABI confirms them in writing.

SABI will provide the service with reasonable care and skill.

Each main written deliverable includes one consolidated revision round and one final minor-corrections check. The application review includes one full review and one minor pre-submission check. Errors introduced by SABI do not use the revision allowance.

A new career direction, additional vacancy, substantially new information supplied after drafting or work outside the package may require a separate agreement and fee. No extra fee will be charged without prior agreement.

3. Client responsibilities and limits

The client should provide information that is accurate and reasonably complete, tell SABI about important changes, and check final documents before using them, particularly names, dates, employers, qualifications and factual claims.

SABI will not knowingly invent or materially misrepresent experience or evidence. SABI is not an employer or recruitment agency and cannot guarantee interviews, job offers, salaries or other employment outcomes.

The package does not include ongoing vacancy searching, weekly job suggestions, unlimited messaging, repeated full application reviews, repeated interview coaching, a formal mock interview or additional CVs and letters for unrelated directions unless separately agreed.

4. Payment and contract

The price is £135 and does not renew automatically. Stripe handles card processing. SABI does not receive or store full card details.

The contract is formed when the £135 payment is successfully completed. SABI will send confirmation of the contract on a durable medium, such as email, before substantive personalised work begins. The confirmation will include or link to information the client can save.

5. Cancellation and starting work early

A consumer purchasing online normally has 14 days from the day after the contract is formed to cancel without giving a reason. The client may cancel by emailing a clear statement to hello@sabigroup.co.uk. The optional cancellation form may also be used.

SABI will not begin substantive personalised work during the cancellation period unless the client separately requests an early start.

Where an early start is requested and the client later cancels before completion, SABI may deduct only a fair and proportionate amount for work actually supplied. If the client has requested an early start, acknowledged the consequences and the service is fully completed during the cancellation period, the statutory cancellation right may end once the service has been fully performed.

If no early start is requested, substantive work will begin after the cancellation period ends. If the client cancels before substantive work begins, the payment will be refunded in full. A statutory cancellation refund will normally be made within 14 days after SABI is informed, using the original payment method unless another lawful method is agreed.

6. Communication, privacy and concerns

Routine communication will normally take place by email. WhatsApp may be used for brief practical messages and during the included follow-up period. Calls and meetings are not automatically recorded. Any recording requires advance notice and separate agreement.

The client may use, copy, edit and submit the final personalised documents for their own career, employment, education or volunteering purposes. SABI retains its rights in its general templates, branding and service materials.

The Privacy Policy explains how personal information is used. Questions or complaints can be sent to hello@sabigroup.co.uk. Nothing in these terms removes rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.
