STEP 8

Working together

What happens after you send this form

  1. I will review and work through the information you have shared.
  2. If I need anything else, I will send follow-up questions in a form. You can reply through the form or through WhatsApp messages or voice notes, whichever is most practical. This gives us both a clear record of what you have shared.
  3. I will research and write up suitable roles and career directions you could consider.
  4. You can then have an optional short phone or video call to discuss the findings, explore anything in more detail and agree what your CVs should be aimed towards. If you do not want a call, we can cover this in writing or voice notes instead.
