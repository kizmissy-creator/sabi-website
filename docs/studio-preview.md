# SABI Studio storefront preview

Route: `/studio/`. Static HTML/CSS/JS served by the existing Netlify publish root. Branch: `feat/sabi-studio-storefront`, draft PR #3. Public home and client routes are unchanged.

## Current direction

The 17 October rebuild replaces the generic boundaries-led storefront with a founder-led introduction, real product imagery, the mask crop, and a warmer storybook influence drawn from Sam's Scenic Route brief. See `studio-direction.md` for source context and superseded decisions.

Header/footer use the small original cream-and-teal clothing mark, with readable SABI Studio text. Products: seven I Wear My Mask All Year Round pieces alongside four Ghost Mode pieces. Native dialogs explain each product's status. Founder portrait already exists on the public site; the worn hoodie sample comes from the product-review material.

Mask clothing and tote use the selected Inkthreadable garment images with the original artwork at planned print sizes, including the hero crop. They are labelled product mockups and show complete garment shapes without editor guides. Vertical placement is recreated from saved drafts; final fit, colour and placement await physical sample checks. Ghost Mode tee, beanie and tote retain their design mockups. The Scenic Route drawing is a small website illustration of the idea, not production artwork.

## Preview boundaries

Orders are closed and the page is noindex. No cart, checkout, payment, analytics or order submission is added. Prices, sizes, supplier variants, fabric/feel information, production times, delivery and returns must be verified before opening. The washed crop is planned with Inkthreadable, front artwork only and no back logo; the Ghost Mode range is planned through Printful.

Publish through the existing GitHub PR Netlify Deploy Preview. Do not merge to production without instruction.

## 18 October collection expansion

The catalogue now contains seven mask products and four Ghost Mode products in two linked collection sections. Mask clothing is grouped separately from the tote and art prints. Product dialogs show the relevant image and status-specific description; the mini print also shows its refined reverse design. The later image-consistency correction replaced the mask AI model previews and framed screenshots with native supplier compositions. No selling prices are displayed. The fitted tee is included because a draft exists; the uncreated baby tee and abandoned stickers are not included. See studio-direction.md for source assets, selected suppliers and saved print dimensions.
