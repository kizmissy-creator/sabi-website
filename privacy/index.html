Privacy, in plain English

Website & enquiry privacy

What this website loads, what a short enquiry would contain, and how that information is intended to be used.

Review draft · 14 October 2026 · Version enquiries-draft-2026-10-14

This is a review draft.

The enquiry form in this preview is disabled and has no submission endpoint. Typing into it does not send an enquiry to SABI. The website host and external resources may still receive ordinary connection information when you view pages.

This draft does not activate a service, authorise new AI processing or certify that the proposed deletion periods are operating. It must be checked against the approved live configuration before enquiries are enabled. Detailed client forms, payments and bookings need their own appropriate information at the point they are used.

What a short enquiry would collect

  • Your name and email address.
  • Your selected service area, starting-point choice and brief message.
  • Your optional preferred communication method.
  • A submission reference, attempt reference, timing information, website origin and the version of the privacy information shown.

Name, email and a brief message are needed to respond meaningfully. Without them, SABI cannot handle the enquiry through the form. Communication preference is optional.

No uploads, payment details, identity documents or detailed medical, legal or financial evidence are requested here. Please leave out unnecessary information about yourself or anyone else. A private route is offered separately where an agreed service needs more information.

Why the information would be used

Your requested enquiry

To understand what you need, respond, assess fit and prepare requested next steps or a quotation. Where you ask about a possible service, the intended basis is taking steps at your request before a possible contract.

Security and proportionate records

To prevent misuse, recognise repeated submissions and organise the enquiry. The intended basis is SABI’s legitimate interest in operating a secure, reliable service and keeping proportionate records. The purpose is not marketing or profiling.

Applicable legal obligations may require limited records to be retained, for example for a dispute or a data-rights request. The final notice must identify the basis that applies to each actual processing activity.

Your right to object

You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact SABI using the privacy address. SABI will consider your circumstances and explain any continuing lawful reason for the processing.

Where information would go

The intended enquiry route uses Google Workspace: Google Apps Script processes the submission and a restricted Google Sheet holds the enquiry record. SABI uses business email to respond. Access is limited to Sam unless another person is specifically authorised.

Netlify hosts the website. An enabled form would send its answers directly from your browser to Google’s receiver, rather than into Netlify site files. GitHub holds website source code, not submitted enquiry records.

SABI does not sell enquiry information or automatically add enquirers to a marketing list. Necessary disclosures may be made where required by law. Ordinary postal correspondence may be handled and scanned by SABI’s virtual-office provider, Virtually There.

Website resources and technical information

Providers may receive IP addresses, browser or connection information and the requested resource when your browser connects to them.

  • Netlify: delivers the website and may handle technical and security records.
  • Google Fonts: the current homepage requests font styles and files from Google’s font services.
  • Tailwind CDN: the current homepage requests a styling script from cdn.tailwindcss.com.
  • Google Apps Script: a receiver connection would be made only after the form is enabled and submitted.

The new service and privacy pages use local styling and locally hosted images. The reviewed source contains no optional analytics, advertising tags, session replay or application-level browser storage. This is a source-code observation, not a completed audit of provider cookies or logs.

Before activation, the live resource, cookie and similar-technology audit must identify each provider, purpose and duration. Optional tracking remains outside the approved launch.

International processing

Google and Netlify may process information outside the UK. The existing systems assessment identifies provider data-processing terms and UK transfer safeguards for review. The final notice must confirm which safeguards apply to SABI’s actual accounts and services, and explain how you can obtain further information or a copy where appropriate.

No assurance of UK-only storage is made in this draft. You can ask SABI about the applicable arrangements using the privacy address.

Retention: the plan and the remaining check

The existing policy intends to keep general enquiries, Free Options Chat notes and unused quotations for normally three months after the last meaningful contact when no service is purchased. A longer, limited period may apply to an active complaint, dispute or legal obligation.

That intended period has not yet been verified across the actual mailbox, enquiry Sheet, backups and provider retention settings. Provider retention may preserve covered records after ordinary deletion. Before live collection, SABI must reconcile the intended period with those settings and test the deletion process. This draft does not promise that information is irreversibly erased after three months.

If an enquiry becomes a service, the relevant service notice must explain its separate records and retention rules.

AI, choices and your rights

This preview does not send enquiry answers to an AI system. It does not give agents permission to inspect client records. Any future AI use with identifiable information requires a separately approved purpose, product, account and controls.

SABI does not make solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about clients. Human review remains part of assessing service fit and delivering client-facing work.

Depending on the purpose and lawful basis, you may ask to access, correct, erase or restrict information, object to legitimate-interest processing, or receive eligible information in a portable format. Where a separate activity relies on consent, you can withdraw it for future processing. Some rights are subject to legal conditions and continuing lawful retention needs.

This privacy information is not a contract and you do not have to agree to it. Any genuine consent request or contractual choice is separate.

You can contact SABI at privacy@sabigroup.co.uk. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office, whether or not you contact SABI first.
