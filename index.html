A bit about me

I like knowing
how things work.

And why they sometimes don’t.

Sam · Founder of SABI
Newcastle upon Tyne

People, ideas, systems. I’m interested in the connections between them—and why something that looks simple on paper can be so awkward in real life.

My background crosses biomedical science, operations, business support, career guidance, research and writing. SABI brings those interests together in useful, everyday help.

I’ll listen, ask questions and help you make sense of the CV, process or piece of writing in front of you. Your time, energy and circumstances matter too.

There’s usually a thread. I like finding it.

Before you ask

A few things you might be wondering.

Prefer to write things down? Need a bit of time to think? We can start there.

Do I have to have a call?

No. Writing things down is fine. Tell me how you prefer to communicate, and we’ll agree what works for the service.

What if I don’t know exactly what I need?

You don’t have to pick a package first. Tell me what’s getting stuck and what you’d like to be easier. We’ll work out where to start.

Is there lower-cost career support?

Starter CVs are £30 for a relatively straightforward work history. Pay It Forward CV support is also available when funding allows. See the career support options.
