The person behind the work

I’m Sam.
I’m good at finding the thread.

My background brings together biomedical science, operations, business support, career guidance, research and writing.

I look at how the information, the process and the person fit together. Then I help turn the complicated parts into something useful.

Based in Newcastle upon Tyne.

An illustrative example

One booking. Three places to update.

A booking arrives by email. Its details are copied into a spreadsheet, then into a calendar. Changes mean checking all three again.

The useful question:

Where should the information live, and who needs it next?

I’d start by mapping that journey, identifying the repeated steps and agreeing a simpler process you can maintain.

The same habit of looking for the thread helps me organise career experience and make complex writing clearer.

Built around real people

Support should feel easier to use.

Clear language, flexible communication and respect for your time, energy and circumstances are part of the work.

Do I have to have a call?

No. Written alternatives are available. You can share your communication preference in your enquiry, and we can agree what works for the service.

What if I don’t know exactly what I need?

You don’t need to choose a package first. Briefly explain what is getting stuck and what you would like to be easier. We can work out a suitable starting point together.

Is there lower-cost career support?

Starter CVs are £30 for a relatively straightforward work history. Pay It Forward CV support is also available when funding allows. See the career support options.
